# NISM Series VIII — Equity Derivatives: Study Plan (2-4 weeks part-time)

**Exam:** 100 MCQs, 2 hours, 60% pass, negative marking 25%. Official workbook ~15 chapters.

## Week 1 — Foundations
- [ ] Ch 1-3: Derivatives basics, forwards/futures, payoff diagrams
- [ ] Ch 4-5: Options terminology, moneyness, payoffs
- Do 50 practice Qs, log in `mock_test_log.csv`

## Week 2 — Pricing & Margins
- [ ] Ch 6-8: Cost-of-carry, Black-Scholes intuition, Greeks (delta/gamma/theta/vega)
- [ ] Ch 9-10: Margins (SPAN, exposure, MTM), VaR, collateral
- Mock 1 (full 100 Qs) — target 65%+

## Week 3 — Clearing, Settlement, Regulations
- [ ] Ch 11-13: Clearing corp, settlement cycle (T+1), exercise/assignment
- [ ] Ch 14-15: SEBI regulations, position limits, risk management
- Mock 2 — target 75%+, review weak chapters

## Week 4 — Revision + Booking
- [ ] Full workbook skim, formula sheet (futures pricing, margins, Greeks)
- [ ] Mock 3 — target 80%+
- [ ] Book exam at nism.ac.in, 3 days prior light review only

## Daily habit (30-45 min)
- 20 Qs from workbook, update `mock_test_log.csv` with score + weak topic.
- Flashcards for margins/settlement dates (these are NISM's favorite traps).
